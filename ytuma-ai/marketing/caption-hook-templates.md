# Caption & Hook Templates

Generic, reusable templates — no client-specific content. Fill in the bracketed parts per post.

## Hook types (pick ONE per post, per the strategy doc)

1. **How-to** — "How I built an AI that answers my phone while I'm at a showing."
2. **Statement** — "Your assistant shouldn't need to sleep. Mine doesn't."
3. **Benefit (no pain framing)** — "Every missed call becomes a booked appointment. Automatically."
4. **Fact/stat** — "The average agent loses 15 hours a week to scheduling and admin."
5. **Negative/pain-avoidance** — "Stop losing leads because you couldn't answer in time."
6. **Personal/emotional** — "I used to dread checking my inbox. Now it checks itself."
7. **Story/anecdotal** — "A client texted at 11pm asking about a showing. My AI answered before I even saw it."

## Caption skeleton (feed post / reel)

```
[Hook — one of the 7 types above, one sentence]

[One concrete detail: what specifically happened / what it does — no fluff]

[Why it matters to the viewer, phrased as their problem, not your feature]

[Soft CTA: "DM me 'DEMO' to see how this works for your business" / "Link in bio to book a 15-min call"]
```

## Platform-specific rewrite prompts

Use these to adapt one piece of source content (a script, a case study, a feature description) across
platforms. Paste your source text where indicated.

**Instagram caption prompt:**
"Turn this into an Instagram caption. Pull out a problem, a fact, and a benefit. Tone: like you're having a
thought and just writing it down — no emojis, no hashtags. One coherent short paragraph. End with a
question that invites a comment or DM.
[paste source text]"

**LinkedIn post prompt:**
"Turn this into a LinkedIn post. Pull out a problem, a fact, and a benefit. Tone: professional and
informational, no emojis, no hashtags. Start with a headline-style first line, add one practical insight,
end with a question that invites professional discussion.
[paste source text]"

**Reel script / voiceover prompt:**
"Turn this into a 30-second reel script. Open with a hook (state which of the 7 hook types to use). Keep
sentences short enough to read aloud in one breath. End on the CTA: [DM keyword / link in bio].
[paste source text]"

## Hook-extraction prompt (for repurposing long-form content)

Use this when you have a longer video/post and want to mine it for short-form hooks:

"You are an expert copywriter. Extract the hook from this script — the first sentence(s) meant to grab
attention. Give me the original hook, then 6 alternates using these types: how-to, statement, benefit
(no pain), fact/stat, negative-framed, personal/emotional, story/anecdotal. Format as: Hook 0 (original),
Hook 1 (How-to), Hook 2 (Statement), Hook 3 (Benefit), Hook 4 (Fact), Hook 5 (Negative), Hook 6 (Personal),
Hook 7 (Story).
[paste script]"

## CTA library (rotate, don't reuse the same one every post)

- "DM me 'DEMO' and I'll show you exactly how this works."
- "Link in bio if you want this built for your business."
- "Comment 'BUSY' if your inbox looks like this too."
- "Book a free 15-minute call — link in bio."
