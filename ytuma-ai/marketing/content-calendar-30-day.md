## Notes on filling this in with real content

- Every "Proof" slot needs an actual screen recording — don't mock these up. If you don't have footage yet,
  swap that day for a "Behind-the-build" or "Problem" post instead.
- Any "Social proof" slot requires the client's written permission before posting anything traceable to
  them, even anonymized numbers if the volume is small enough to identify them.
- Repeat this 30-day cycle monthly, replacing 30–40% of slots with fresh proof content as you build more
  client deployments; keep the pillar ratio roughly constant.
